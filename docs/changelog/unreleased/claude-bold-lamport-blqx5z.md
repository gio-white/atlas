- **2026-11-14 —** `habits-p1-model` — Habits brief phase 1, the model ([habits brief](../../plans/habits-brief.md), [decision 0010](../../decisions/0010-habits-rebuilt-as-rules-marks-and-freezes.md)). A habit no longer has a metric, kind, or target: it has rule versions (`habit_rule`: how often — every day, specific days, times a week or a month — and how it is checked — ticked by you, or from your data: wake time, bedtime, time asleep, Bad apps, one category, one app, all screen time, sessions planned), marks (`habit_mark`: Done or Not done for a day), and freezes (`habit_freeze`, for one habit or all, replacing skips and pauses). Schema 15 converts each old habit into its first rule plus one Done mark per day the old rule met, turns breaks into freezes, archives the metrics habits read (entries kept), and drops `habit_break`; older exports convert the same way on import. Screen devices gain `complete_through` ("complete up to"), set by imports, the API, and the seed. New API: `GET` / `POST /habits`, `GET` / `PATCH` / `DELETE /habits/{slug}` (`PATCH` takes `apply`: `from_today` or `all_days`), archive and restore, `PUT` / `DELETE /habits/{slug}/days/{day}`, `GET` / `POST /freezes`, `DELETE /freezes/{id}`; the old status, detail, check-in, break, and grid routes are gone. The demo seed has twelve habits covering every rule, a rule change, an archived habit, and two freezes. Until the new screens land, Habits (`/habits`) and Home's Habits block are read-only lists, `/habit`, `/habit/:slug`, and `/week` redirect to `/habits`, and Habits kept reads "—". Tests: `tests/services/test_habits.py`, `tests/domain/test_habit_migration.py`, `tests/db/test_habit_migration.py`, `tests/api/test_habits.py`, `web/src/lib/habits.test.ts`, `web/src/pages/HabitsPage.test.tsx`.

- **2026-11-14 —** `habits-p2-calculations` — Habits brief phase 2, the calculations ([habits](../../domain/habits.md#day-results-streaks-and-habits-kept)). Each day of each habit is computed from your marks, its freezes, and (for a data habit) a reading from sleep, screen time, or sessions: Not scheduled, Upcoming, Frozen, Done, Not done, Open or In progress (today), Waiting for data (yesterday), or Blank (a weekly or monthly habit's day without a tick), with reasons No data received, Data incomplete, Waiting for data, Over the limit, Set by you, Frozen. Missing or incomplete data is never Done; screen time is complete only when every active device covers the day. Periods follow rule versions (a weekly count change applies to the week that contains it; every day → N× a week starts a partial week) with the proportional target `ceil(N × open days ÷ days)`. Current and best streak in the habit's own period, Kept last 4 weeks, and Habits kept (min(Done, target) over targets) are now computed, so Home and Stats → This week show Habits kept again and This week's habit rows carry `done` / `target`. New views: `GET /views/habits/day`, `GET /views/habits/week`, `GET /views/habit/{slug}`. The old period helpers moved to `domain/caps.py` for screen caps. Tests: `tests/domain/test_habits.py` (every case in brief §6), `tests/services/test_habit_views.py`, `tests/domain/test_caps.py`, `tests/api/test_habits.py`.
